About the Journey experience

Journey brings together experiences designed for those who move, explore, and let the landscape set the pace. From quiet forest paths to demanding mountain routes, each journey is defined not by distance, but by the feeling of being in motion.

Forest walks & nature trails

Under the canopy, pace slows to the rhythm of roots and light.

  • Redwoods Treewalk (Whakarewarewa Forest)
  • Otaneiwainuku Scenic Reserve
  • Waitākere Ranges
  • Karangahake Gorge
  • Te Ara Paparārangi

Hiking & multi-day routes

Elevation and distance matter less than the pull of the next ridge.
