# Project Overview

## 1. What is PurePods

PurePods is a collection of unique accommodation experiences set in the natural landscapes of New Zealand. It is not a traditional hotel, nor a standard tourist accommodation.

The PurePods experience is based on a deep and personal immersion in nature, designed to slow down, disconnect and experience the landscape in an intimate and mindful way.

PurePods are glass eco-cabins, fully integrated into their surroundings, allowing guests to experience nature through all five senses — light, sound, weather, night skies and the ever-changing landscape — even from inside the pod.

## 2. Core Experience

The PurePods experience is defined by:

- complete privacy and true seclusion
- a direct connection with nature
- calm, silence and a strong sense of refuge
- minimalist, functional design that respects the environment
- stargazing directly from bed
- an experience primarily designed for couples

Although nature is always present (rain, wind, cold, heat), guests remain safe, warm and comfortable, without losing their connection to the outside world.

## 3. Sustainability

Sustainability is an essential part of the PurePods concept:

- responsible use of resources
- sensitive integration with the land
- collaboration with local landowners to support conservation and protect natural landscapes

## 4. Website Intent

The website must convey exclusivity, simplicity and respect for the environment.

It should never feel commercial or promotional, but calm, honest and experience-led.

## 5. Website Goals

### Overall Goal

The PurePods website should not behave like a traditional tourism or accommodation website.

Its primary purpose is to **awaken desire**, not to push a booking.

The site should invite visitors to imagine themselves there, to slow down, and to feel that this place fits a meaningful moment in their lives — whether it’s a celebration, a shared escape, or simply the need to pause.

---

### Inspire Before Selling

The website must **inspire before it converts**.

- Inspiration comes before price, availability or urgency
- The experience is shown, not over-explained
- Visitors should arrive at the desire to book on their own

Booking is never forced — it appears only once the desire already exists.

---

### Make PurePods Easy to Understand

Within a few seconds, visitors should clearly understand that:

- PurePods is not a hotel
- It is not mass tourism
- It is an intimate, considered experience
- It is primarily designed for couples
- It takes place in real, secluded and carefully chosen locations

The website should remove any confusion from the very first interaction.

---

### Calm and Intuitive Exploration

Navigation should feel simple, clear and frictionless.

- Exploring the pods should feel like a gentle journey, not a search
- There should be no overload of options or decisions
- Visitors should be able to discover pods by:
  - location
  - atmosphere
  - type of experience
  - mood

The website guides the visitor — it never pushes them.

---

### Meaningful Experiences

PurePods is not booked casually.

The website should subtly suggest that this is the place to:

- celebrate an anniversary
- disconnect after an intense period
- give a meaningful gift
- live something memorable, not just consumable

It should create anticipation without making explicit promises.

---

### Quiet Luxury

PurePods represents luxury — but not an unreachable or artificial one.

Luxury is understood as:

- silence
- space
- time
- privacy
- genuine connection with nature

Never as excess, display or elitism.

The website should communicate a **human, honest and attainable luxury**.

---

### Natural Path to Booking

Booking should feel like a natural final step, not a forced goal.

- No urgency messaging
- No aggressive calls to action
- No sense of being sold to

Booking should feel like a personal and conscious decision.

---

> The website should make people want to go, not tell them why they should.

## 6. User Experience Principles

The experience of navigating the website should reflect the experience of staying at a PurePod.

The website should feel calm, intuitive and unhurried, allowing visitors to explore at their own pace and enjoy the content without effort.

### Key Principles

- Simplicity over complexity, both in structure and navigation
- Slow, considered exploration, avoiding information or visual overload
- Imagery leads the experience; text supports and provides context
- Clear, predictable navigation with no friction at any point
- Conscious use of space, rhythm and visual silence to convey calm

The website should be easy to use for all types of users, regardless of age or digital confidence.

The overall experience should create a sense of wellbeing throughout the visit.
